How the baseline is priced

The baseline prices the unique tokens we removed at the cache-write rate they would have entered as (~11.5× a cache read on a prompt-caching backend), and the re-sent remainder at the cache-read rate the provider would have served it from. Pricing gross savings as writes is how a dashboard overstates itself by its own overcount ratio — which is why dollar savings and token savings diverge so sharply on this workload.

Cold storage, and the † on a dollar figure

A transcript that has moved to cold storage is fetched only when you ask for it. A † means some of that session's turns carried no usable provider usage data, so the dollar figure covers its priced turns only — while the token columns beside it cover every turn.
